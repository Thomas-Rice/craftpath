Everything between you and 300

  • Live auction house pricesOne click at the auctioneer reads every listing, so the plan uses today's prices on your realm.
  • The cheapest route, recipe by recipeIt weighs every recipe, the chance of a skill-up at each colour, and whether to buy or craft each material.
  • Buys the shopping list for youIt finds each item at the best price and keeps track of what you've bought. You confirm every purchase.
  • Tells you what to craft nextA helper beside the profession window opens the right recipe and moves on to the next one as soon as you pass it.
  • Uses what you already haveMaterials in your bags, bank and mailbox come off the list before anything is bought, and if you have Mining, ore you can smelt counts as bars.
  • Finds what's profitable to craft todayCraftPath Profits lists what you can make for a profit at today's prices, allowing for prices moving, and where to get the recipes you don't have yet.
  • Keeps a history of what worksEverything you buy, sell and craft is recorded, with auction sales read from your mail, so you can see your profit by item, by day or for all time.
  • Stops you selling what you needItems your routes still need are marked in your bags and bank, and it warns you before they go to a vendor or the auction house.
  • Knows where every recipe comes fromTrainers, vendors and where to find them, Merchant's Favor and quest rewards, for your faction.

Get CraftPath

Free · works for Horde and Alliance

Download the addon
  1. Download the zip above.
  2. Unzip it into World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns, so you have a CraftPath folder there.
  3. Start the game, open the auction house and click Scan in the CraftPath window, or type /cpath.

Source code and changes are on GitHub.

Kept up to date every day

CraftPath checks the WoW: Forever game data daily. When recipes are added or changed, the addon and planner are updated, and the changes are listed.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. CraftPath is a free fan project.

Does it play the game for me?

No. It plans, finds items and keeps track, but the game needs you to click for every purchase and every craft, and CraftPath works within that.

Which professions does it cover?

Alchemy, Blacksmithing, Enchanting, Engineering, Leatherworking, Tailoring, Mining (smelting), Cooking and First Aid, from 1 to 300. The recipes come straight from the WoW: Forever game data and are checked every day.

Where do the website's prices come from?

From a recent auction house scan, shown with its date on the planner. Your realm will differ a little, so the planner lets you change any price, and the addon always uses your own live scan.

What if a recipe changes in a game update?

The game data is checked every day. Changes go into the addon and the planner, and are listed on the recipe updates page.
